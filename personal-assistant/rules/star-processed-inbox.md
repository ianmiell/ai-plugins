# Processed emails left in the inbox are starred

After a triage run, every thread that this plugin has processed (shown to
Ian in a triage list) and that is still in the inbox must be **starred**.
That includes threads Ian chose to keep, Read later threads (see
read-later-stays-in-inbox.md), threads waiting on someone else, and Act now
threads Ian hasn't dealt with yet.

A star means "seen by the assistant, deliberately kept". An unstarred inbox
thread is new and hasn't been triaged yet.

- Star them in the same execute step as the other approved actions. Ian
  approving the batch covers this; no separate confirmation is needed.
- Don't star threads that weren't part of the batch shown to Ian.
